# Credits, quality, and limits

ObjectRemover uses pay-as-you-go credits with no subscription. You can start free with a watermarked Low quality run, and credits, valid for 12 months, pay for Low, Standard, or Fine quality and downloads without the tool watermark.

All prices and limits on this page were read from [objectremover.video/pricing](https://objectremover.video/pricing), [objectremover.video/upload](https://objectremover.video/upload), and the [homepage](https://objectremover.video/) on November 27, 2026. Check the pricing page for current numbers.

## Is ObjectRemover free?

You can try it free with no credit card. The free option is **Free Low**: a Low quality run meant for small-area jobs. Large masks and 1080p or higher footage need Standard or Fine. A Free Low result always has the ObjectRemover tool watermark burned in. You can download that watermarked file at no cost.

## Can I export without a watermark for free?

No. A file without the ObjectRemover tool watermark needs credits. There are two ways to get one:

1. Run Free Low, then spend credits to get a clean copy of that result.
2. Choose Standard or Fine before processing. Credits are charged up front and the export has no tool watermark.

Note that the tool watermark is ObjectRemover's own mark on free output. It is a different thing from erasing a logo that is already in footage you own.

## How many credits does a job use?

Each job is charged a base amount plus an amount for duration, adjusted for resolution and quality. Longer clips, higher resolution, and Fine quality use more credits. **You see the exact quote before the job starts**, after you confirm the mask, so you can trim the clip or pick a lower tier first. The pricing page gives rough guides, for example that 100 Basic credits cover about 8 to 10 short removals at typical Standard quality, but the quote on your clip is what counts.

## What are daily preview confirms?

Before processing, you confirm what will be removed. Each successful describe or brush selection counts as one confirm. The daily limit depends on your pack (see the table). Plus and higher keep their higher limit while the credit balance is above 30 credits. If the balance drops to 30 or below, the daily limit temporarily matches Basic until you top up.

## Do credits expire? Is there a subscription?

There is no subscription and no recurring charge. Credits expire 12 months after purchase. Credits usually appear in your account right after payment. Unused credits from a recent purchase can be refunded within 14 days under the [refund policy](https://objectremover.video/refund).

## What are the quality tiers?

| Low (free) | Free, small-area jobs | Always included |
| Low (paid) | Credits | None after paying for a clean copy |
| Standard | Credits | None |
| Fine | Credits | None |

## What are the file limits?

| Limit | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Video formats | MP4, MOV |
| Video file size | up to 100 MB |
| Video frame rate | up to 60 fps |
| Photo formats | JPG, PNG, WebP |
| Photo file size | up to 20 MB |
| Upload method | File picker or drag and drop; no paste-URL |

The upload page states the current processing window for free and paid accounts. Trim long clips to the section you need before uploading.

## How long are results kept?

Paid and clean outputs are kept about 7 days. Free watermarked previews are kept about 1 day. After that they are deleted and cannot be recovered, so download promptly.

## Do the same credits work in Premiere Pro?

Yes. The [Premiere Pro plugin](premiere-pro-plugin.md) signs in to the same account and uses the same balance. Credits are bought on the website.
