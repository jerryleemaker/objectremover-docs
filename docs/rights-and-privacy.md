# Rights and privacy

Use ObjectRemover only on videos and photos you own or are allowed to edit, and never to remove other people's credits, watermarks, or branding. ObjectRemover states that uploads are not used to train AI models, and processed files are deleted after a short retention period.

Checked against the [homepage](https://objectremover.video/), [features page](https://objectremover.video/features), [about page](https://objectremover.video/about), and [privacy policy](https://objectremover.video/privacy) on November 27, 2026. The privacy policy and [terms of service](https://objectremover.video/terms) are the binding documents. This page is a plain-language summary.

## What footage am I allowed to edit?

Footage you own, or footage you have permission to change. Typical legitimate cases:

- Removing a passer-by from your own travel clip
- Cleaning a timestamp your camera burned into your own shoot
- Taking an outdated name tag or lower third off your own interview
- Removing captions you burned into your own export
- Clearing your own channel logo from a master before reposting it elsewhere
- Fixing an AI-generated clip whose terms let you edit it

## What should I not use it for?

- Removing credits, copyright notices, watermarks, logos, or branding from someone else's work
- Re-uploading other people's content as your own
- Editing material in a way that misrepresents a real event, especially news or evidence
- Anything that breaks the platform rules where you publish

ObjectRemover does not accept a pasted video URL. You upload a file from your own device. Content that violates the terms of service may be removed and accounts may be suspended.

## Are my uploads used to train AI models?

No. ObjectRemover states that uploaded videos, processed videos, and anything you create with the tool are not used to train, fine-tune, or improve its AI models. Your content is used only to process your request.

## How long are my files kept?

As of November 2026:

| File | Kept for about |
| --- | --- |
| Paid or clean (watermark-free) outputs | 7 days |
| Free watermarked previews, and any clean copy held in case you pay for it | 1 day |

After that, files are deleted and cannot be recovered. Download your results promptly and keep your own originals.

## Does anyone look at my videos?

The privacy policy says processing is automatic and staff do not view or review your video content unless required by law or legal process.

## What personal data does ObjectRemover collect?

The privacy policy lists account basics such as email address and, if you sign in with Google, profile information, plus settings and project metadata. Payments go through Creem as Merchant of Record, and ObjectRemover receives transaction status and billing email, not full card details. Optional analytics cookies require consent, and you can change cookie choices at any time from the cookie icon on the site.

## What rights do I have over my data?

The privacy policy lists deleting your account and associated data, exporting your data, clearing local storage, managing cookie preferences, and contacting a person about privacy. It also references GDPR and CCPA.

## Is a reconstructed area real footage?

No. Inpainting creates a plausible background. It does not recover what was actually hidden. Keep an untouched original and disclose substantial edits where that matters.

## Who do I contact?

support@objectremover.video for product, billing, or privacy questions.
