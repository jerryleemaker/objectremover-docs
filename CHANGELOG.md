# Changelog

This changelog records public, dated changes to ObjectRemover ([objectremover.video](https://objectremover.video/)) and to this documentation. It starts in November 2026. Earlier entries are limited to changes announced in dated posts on the [ObjectRemover blog](https://objectremover.video/blog), and each entry links its source.

## How do I find out what changed in ObjectRemover?

Read this file from the top. Product entries link to the official announcement. For current prices and limits, the [pricing page](https://objectremover.video/pricing) and [upload page](https://objectremover.video/upload) are always the source of truth.

## 2026-11-27: Documentation published

- First version of this repository: getting started, four use-case guides, Premiere Pro plugin, credits and limits, failure cases, comparisons, FAQ, and rights and privacy.
- Snapshot of the product as documented on objectremover.video on this date:
  - Uploads: MP4 and MOV up to 100 MB (up to 60 fps); JPG, PNG, and WebP up to 20 MB; no paste-URL.
  - Flow: describe in chat or brush, confirm the mask, preview, download. Remove or extract.
  - Free Low output always includes an ObjectRemover tool watermark. Paid credits give Low, Standard, or Fine quality and downloads without the tool watermark.
  - Credit packs: Custom from $3.87, Basic $9.99 for 100, Plus $19.99 for 225, Premium $29.99 for 400, Enterprise $99.99 for 1500. No subscription. Credits valid 12 months. Quote shown before each job.
  - Retention: paid outputs about 7 days, free watermarked previews about 1 day. Uploads are not used for AI training.
- Comparison facts for Adobe Generative Remove (beta), Runway Remove from Video, and CapCut AI remove accessed on this date.

## 2026-11-10: Brush or type selection guide

- The official guide [Brush or type to remove objects from video](https://objectremover.video/blog/brush-or-type-remove-objects-from-video) documents both selection paths: typing a description in chat, or brushing over the target on the preview, followed by mask confirmation.

## 2026-10-12: Premiere Pro plugin

- ObjectRemover became available as a panel inside Adobe Premiere Pro 25.6 and newer, on macOS and Windows, installed from a single `.ccx` file.
- The panel removes or extracts a confirmed selection and returns the result on a new track. Processing runs in the ObjectRemover cloud using the same account and credits as the website. Credits are bought on the website.
- Source: [ObjectRemover Premiere Pro plugin is live](https://objectremover.video/blog/premiere-pro-plugin-object-removal); details on the [plugin page](https://objectremover.video/plugins/premiere-pro).

## 2026-06-05: Multi-object removal and extraction

- One request can now select every instance of a category (for example "penguins" or "cars") or several different targets, and remove or extract them in one pass, on both photos and videos.
- Source: [Multi-object removal and extraction is live](https://objectremover.video/blog/multi-object-removal-extraction-live).
